# Choose the capability taxonomy and enforcement contract

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Parent: [Design and launch reusable Restricted Group Policy](../map.md)
Blocked by: 01

## Question

What capability vocabulary, declaration contract, unknown-capability state, and per-operation authority-check semantics will let policies default-allow classified reads while reliably denying owner-private data and all non-exempt writes? Decide how direct and indirect capabilities compose, how newly installed tools/plugins/MCP servers become classified, how revocation reaches in-flight tasks immediately, and how the design remains profile-safe and prompt-cache safe.

## Answer

1. The authorization unit is a structured `Capability Request`, derived from a fixed Core `Capability Descriptor` envelope plus validated typed extensions and trusted principal, origin, policy, task, target, and parent-operation context; tool names and prose are evidence rather than authority. See the shared terms in [`CONTEXT.md`](../../../CONTEXT.md) and the reusable boundary inventory in [the enforcement-seam research](../research/01-enforcement-seams.md).
2. Authenticated owner requests retain full owner authority in every conversation; non-owner members of exact owner-designated Control Channels receive full operational authority but never owner identity or governance, while non-owner requests elsewhere use the exact group's Restricted Group Policy. Multi-member plaintext credential/auth egress is always denied. Ordinary non-owner Control Channel PII/NDA egress requires owner release, while an owner-designated Approval Surface may display the exact sensitive decision payload of a pending approval request without unrelated context.
3. Any number of Restricted Groups may coexist, each with an independent exact group fingerprint, effective policy revision, grants, elevations, storage, history, and audit; no approval or modification propagates to another group or profile implicitly.
4. Requests carry all applicable orthogonal effects and monotonic provenance/sensitivity labels, every effect must be authorized, and derived data inherits all source labels without implicit declassification; `secret_exposure` is ungrantable and policy governance is owner-only.
5. A classified operation follows its group's explicit rules and baseline, an ambiguous identity/target or hard-denied effect fails closed, and an unknown operation pauses. Missing descriptors may produce an `Inferred Capability Proposal`, but inference never executes until the owner approves an exact function and resource scope for that group and implementation/version fingerprint.
6. A persistent `Capability Grant` is exact-group, exact-function, and exact-resource scoped until owner revocation; owner-private reads remain denied by default but may receive an exact-durable-task-only `Temporary Elevation` that survives process restart and expires only when the task completes, aborts, or merges, or when the owner revokes it. It never exposes credentials or transfers to a merged target task.
7. Every direct and indirect executable boundary creates a child request whose authority is the intersection of parent authority, current policy revision, matching grant/elevation, and Core guards, so plugins, MCP, shell/scripts, providers, subagents, delayed work, and egress cannot inherit blanket permission.
8. Policies use immutable per-group revisions and atomic current-revision reads at each boundary. An operation already started may finish after revocation; its egress is checked separately, blocks sensitive or unknown data, permits only a one-time exact-origin return of non-sensitive output, and records both start and egress revisions.
9. Policy/grant state is active-profile-local under `HERMES_HOME`; no cross-profile inheritance exists. The descriptor envelope and tool schema stay byte-stable for the session, while runtime policy lookup applies approvals/revocations without rebuilding the system prompt; newly installed tool schemas wait until a new session.
10. Restricted-group use may emit only de-identified `Optimization Records` automatically; content-bearing `Shared Knowledge Candidates` retain provenance and require owner approval before PKB promotion, and group turns never receive skill-write, provider-source-write, PKB-write, or declassification authority.
11. The production gate may carry exact owner-approved `Owner Risk Exceptions` for fully evidenced implementation defects. This control-plane acceptance can permit first activation or direct resume of the affected exact build, but it is not a Capability Grant: member requests still pass this deterministic contract, unknown requests still pause, prohibited effects remain denied, and missing evidence is not an exception. See [the production go-live decision](04-decide-production-go-live-gate.md).

## Comments

- Grilling Q1: the canonical authorization unit is a structured `Capability Request` matched by policy selectors. Tool/toolset names and provider-defined permission strings are evidence about the request, not the security vocabulary.
- Grilling Q2: every `Capability Descriptor` uses a fixed Core semantic envelope plus validated namespaced typed extensions. Extensions may add policy-selectable detail but may not omit or redefine descriptor identity/version, action, resource and data class, effect class, normalized target scope, authority domain, or trusted policy/origin/principal/task lineage.
- Grilling Q3: a `Capability Request` carries an orthogonal set of effects rather than one primary effect or risk tier. Every effect must be independently authorized; any denied effect denies the request and any unclassified effect makes it unknown, so a read label cannot mask export, egress, mutation, credential, or descendant-authority consequences.
- Grilling Q4: the Core effect vocabulary is `read`, `compute`, `ephemeral_write`, `durable_internal_write`, `external_mutation`, `egress`, `authority_spawn`, `credential_use`, `credential_maintenance`, `policy_admin`, and `secret_exposure`. Credential use and maintenance are separate; `secret_exposure` is an invariant hard deny and `policy_admin` requires owner authority.
- Grilling Q5: data uses an orthogonal monotonic provenance-label set. Every derived value and artifact inherits the union of all input labels plus producer labels; no model, tool, transform, summary, or format conversion implicitly lowers classification. Label removal requires a separately registered and authorized declassifier, and the first version provides none.
- Grilling Q6 (product-level reframe): restricted-group use may automatically persist only de-identified `Optimization Records` containing capability/outcome/feedback evidence. Content-bearing `Shared Knowledge Candidates` require explicit owner approval before entering the owner's PKB. A group turn never gains skill-write, PKB-write, provider-source-write, or declassification authority, and raw Teams/CQ content is not automatically retained under an optimization label.
- Grilling Q7: only the owner may approve an unknown capability. The resulting `Capability Grant` is bound to the exact Restricted Group Policy and therefore only to the requesting Teams group; it never grants the capability to another group, policy, profile, or global Hermes installation. The approval request must reach the owner's exact Teams Control Channel; whether TUI is an additional transport remains part of the separate owner-approval-protocol decision.
- Grilling Q8: within that requesting group, owner approval grants one explicit function and normalized resource scope, such as read-only Jira queries for Project A. It does not approve a whole plugin, adjacent write/admin operations, other projects, credentials, or each request globally; subsequent matching requests remain allowed until the owner revokes the grant.
- Grilling Q9: owner-private sources are denied by default in every non-Control-Channel restricted group, but the owner may issue a `Temporary Elevation` from the exact Control Channel for an explicit function and resource scope requested by one exact group. The elevation does not rewrite the persistent Capability Grant or baseline policy and automatically restores the prior authority when it expires. Credential/secret exposure remains ungrantable; content read under elevation retains provenance, origin-bound egress, and audit requirements.
- Grilling Q10: an authenticated owner request has full owner authority regardless of channel. A non-owner request in an owner-designated exact Control Channel also receives full operational authority, so Control Channels are outside Restricted Group Policy. In every multi-member Control Channel, plaintext credential/auth output remains an invariant hard deny even though providers may use credentials internally. Whether non-owner Control Channel members may also receive owner-private PII/NDA content remains the next explicit decision.
- Grilling Q11: non-owner Control Channel members retain full operational authority, but owner-private PII/NDA content pauses before multi-member egress and requires explicit owner release. Plaintext credential/auth egress is a non-overridable hard deny in every multi-member Teams conversation. Full operational authority does not confer owner identity or owner-only governance: policy changes, Capability Grants, Temporary Elevations, private-content release, and Shared Knowledge Candidate promotion remain owner-only.
- Additional policy-cardinality decision: the framework supports multiple Restricted Groups with independent effective permissions. Each exact tenant/conversation fingerprint binds its own policy/version, Capability Grants, Temporary Elevations, sandbox, history scope, and audit stream; approval or modification for one group never changes another group's effective authority, even if both began from the same template.
- Grilling Q12 (refined by the owner-approval protocol): a Temporary Elevation is bound only to one exact durable task and may authorize repeated matching operations within that task. It survives process restart and expires when the task completes, aborts (including `/stop`), or merges into another task, or on owner revocation. It never transfers to a merged target, queued, or subsequent task, and every operation still re-checks current authority.
- Grilling Q13: every direct and indirect operation is checked at its executable boundary, including nested plugin calls, shell/script actions, MCP operations, provider calls, subagents, delayed work, and egress. A child operation's effective authority is the intersection of its parent's authority, current policy revision, matching grant/elevation, and Core guards; parent approval never widens or blankets child capabilities.
- Grilling Q14: when a newly installed tool/plugin/MCP lacks a valid Capability Descriptor, Hermes derives an explicitly inferred classification proposal from available schema, description, manifest/source evidence, and argument shape instead of requiring the provider to author it first. The proposal is evidence, not authority; whether it may execute before owner review is the next decision, and any approved inference must bind the implementation/version fingerprint so changed code is reclassified.
- Grilling Q15: an inferred classification proposal never grants execution authority. The requesting task pauses until the owner approves the proposed exact function and resource scope through the Control Channel. Approval binds the exact group and implementation/version fingerprint; any fingerprint change invalidates the inference and requires reclassification and owner approval before execution.
- Grilling Q16: revocation does not cancel an operation that has already crossed its executable boundary. That operation may complete and return normally; the next operation re-checks and applies the new authority. Whether a later Teams reply/upload is part of the already-started operation or a separately checked egress boundary remains the next explicit decision.
- Grilling Q17: every reply/upload remains a separately checked egress operation after revocation. PII, NDA, owner-private, secret, policy-control, brokered-confidential, and unknown data are stopped; only non-sensitive output from the already-authorized in-flight operation may make a one-time return to the exact origin group. The next operation uses the new authority. Audit records both operation-start and egress policy revisions and the actual delivery verdict.
- Refinement from ticket 04: the base authorization contract remains deterministic and fail-closed for member requests. A separately authenticated and audited Owner Risk Exception may accept a known defect in the exact deployed implementation, including a defect that can violate the base contract in practice, without converting the prohibited effect into member authority. Unknown or missing evidence cannot receive this exception.
