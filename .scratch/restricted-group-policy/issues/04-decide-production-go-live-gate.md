# Decide the production go-live gate

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Assigned: dev
Parent: [Design and launch reusable Restricted Group Policy](../map.md)
Blocked by:

## Question

After TestGroup unit, integration, adversarial E2E, cleanup, and side-effect read-back all pass, must the owner explicitly approve production activation through a trusted owner surface, or may an automated evidence gate bind the policy to the production Monitor Group? Define the exact evidence bundle, approver identity, activation action, rollback readiness check, and behavior when any evidence is missing or stale.

## Answer

### Owner authority and exact activation proposal

Passing the automated evidence gate never activates a real Monitor Group. It creates one immutable, active-profile-local `Production Activation Proposal` for the authenticated owner to decide through the exact Control Channel or local TUI Approval Surface. The proposal binds the exact program and policy version, implementation fingerprint, effective configuration, TestGroup evidence manifest, production tenant/conversation fingerprint, and either a verified rollback target or an exact accepted exception for a fully evidenced rollback-readiness failure. Any bound change supersedes the proposal and requires the relevant tests plus a new owner decision.

The owner is the sole production governance authority. Non-owner Control Channel operational authority, group membership, display names, model claims, and automation cannot approve, resume, roll back, or disable production policy state.

### Evidence eligibility

The proposal carries an itemized `Activation Evidence Manifest`, not a CI badge. It must contain real test results and independent read-back for all of these categories:

1. Allowed ALPS/MOLY query, attachment, export, and exact-origin return paths.
2. CQ mutation, owner-private/secret/cross-group access or egress, unapproved route, and direct or indirect bypass denials.
3. Approval, restart, cancel, resume, task, descendant, and cross-group isolation behavior.
4. External API side-effect, audit, temporary-storage cleanup, and rollback-restoration read-back.
5. Harmless post-opening production probe definitions and expected allow/deny verdicts, including API, audit, cleanup, and cross-group read-back, for immediate execution after full opening.

A required test that is skipped, executes zero cases, has no result, or lacks required API, audit, cleanup, or rollback evidence is unknown and blocks the proposal. The owner cannot accept an unknown fact. Complete evidence has no calendar-time expiry while the exact program, policy, configuration, profile, test definitions, and test environment remain unchanged. Any such change invalidates it. Production group identity, approved AIDE availability, effective configuration, and rollback recoverability are freshly checked immediately before activation and again at the affected activation step; a mismatch blocks that step.

### Exact owner acceptance of a known defect

The owner may knowingly accept any fully evidenced runtime-requirement failure, including authorization bypass or data exposure, for first activation or later direct resume. Each failure requires its own `Owner Risk Exception` Approval Request showing the exact evidence and consequences and binding the exact profile, production group, policy/build version, function, and failure. The owner chooses an explicit expiry no later than replacement or retirement of that exact version. The exception is audited, independently revocable, never transfers to another failure, version, group, or profile, and never covers a later-discovered or unknown problem.

Every known failure is accepted separately. Only after all exact exceptions are approved does a separate final request ask the owner to activate the whole proposal. Each still-pending exception or final-activation Approval Request uses the approval protocol's 24-hour request deadline; that pending deadline is distinct from the owner-selected lifetime of an exception after approval. Owner authentication, exact scope, consequence disclosure, immutable audit, and revocation are mandatory proof that the exception is the owner's decision; they cannot be bypassed or delegated.

An Owner Risk Exception is not a Capability Grant. It accepts an implementation-defect risk while the base policy continues to deny member requests for CQ mutation, private or secret data, cross-group egress, and every other prohibited effect.

This governance exception explicitly revises prior absolute fail-closed deployment claims: the authenticated owner may accept a fully evidenced implementation-defect risk for one exact activated build. It does not revise the deterministic per-request fail-closed authorization contract or convert a prohibited effect into member authority.

### Progressive production activation

There is no owner-only or harness-only locked production canary. After final owner approval and activation-time checks, the exact production group opens directly to members and the harmless allow/deny production probes run immediately after opening.

Activation is progressive by capability rather than one atomic whole-policy switch. A capability becomes available under the new version after its portion is durably written and read back; until then it uses the prior verified version or remains disabled on first activation. Every admitted operation is pinned to one complete policy version for its whole lifetime and records that version in audit. The operation-start event follows [Define the owner approval protocol](03-define-owner-approval-protocol.md)'s content-minimized lifecycle audit and makes the policy/version fingerprint mandatory; [Define the content-minimized audit contract](11-define-content-minimized-audit-contract.md) owns the final schema. One operation never mixes policy versions.

If Hermes crashes during activation, restart continues the same approved activation only when every bound identity and evidence fingerprint is unchanged, the owner has not revoked it under [Define the owner approval protocol](03-define-owner-approval-protocol.md)'s immutable Owner Decision and revocation-at-next-executable-boundary semantics, production identity/AIDE/configuration/rollback checks still pass, and the durable activation journal proves remaining steps are idempotent and cannot replay a side effect. Otherwise activation stops and requires a new owner decision.

### Post-opening failure handling

`Production Failure Triage` is failure containment only. It does not modify [Choose the capability taxonomy and enforcement contract](02-choose-capability-taxonomy-and-enforcement-contract.md)'s deterministic per-request fail-closed authorization: every member request still requires the applicable Capability Grant or Temporary Elevation regardless of the triage result.

Post-opening probes never auto-roll back the whole binding. A demonstrated failed function is blocked from new operations and from releasing pending output while other functions continue, and the owner receives the evidence.

When the affected scope is uncertain, an approved AIDE-routed LLM classifies the most likely affected function from the current error evidence. Its own reported confidence is the control threshold: strictly greater than 90 percent immediately blocks the inferred function and notifies the owner; 90 percent or lower holds only the triggering operation, direct child work, uncommitted side effects, and unreleased output for an owner decision. Other tasks continue. The inference, confidence, and source evidence are audited. Failure to obtain an inference is low confidence.

A low-confidence hold expires after 24 hours: the operation and child work are cancelled, unreleased output is discarded, uncommitted side effects remain blocked, and task storage is cleaned. This hold uses [Define the owner approval protocol](03-define-owner-approval-protocol.md)'s operation-bound Approval Request, durable outbox, and expiry semantics; expiry emits the same safe `expired` status to the exact origin group without creating a denial rule. A later retry is a new operation. The owner may stop one inferred function, stop all restricted-group work, resume the held operation, or roll back. Resuming a function despite a confirmed defect uses an exact Owner Risk Exception; a false-positive or uncertain one-operation resume remains an exact audited owner decision.

### Rollback readiness and execution

The normal activation baseline requires Hermes to verify an exact recoverable last-known-good policy, configuration, and binding. First activation uses a verified disabled state as its rollback target. A backup that has not been exercised and read back is unknown evidence and blocks activation. If tests instead fully prove a specific rollback-readiness failure—such as confirmed absence of restore authority—the sole owner may accept that known deficiency through its own Owner Risk Exception; the proposal must then state the exact unavailable recovery action and remaining disable/compensation path rather than claim rollback readiness.

Rollback is owner-decided rather than automatically triggered by a failed post-opening probe. The rollback decision uses [Define the owner approval protocol](03-define-owner-approval-protocol.md)'s first-wins immutable Owner Decision semantics; a later opposite intent creates a new governance request rather than rewriting an executed rollback. Once chosen—or when an Owner Risk Exception expires or is revoked—new operations immediately use the verified prior version or disabled state. Already-admitted new-version operations drain only to their next safe executable boundary; unexecuted mutation, egress, and sensitive output are blocked, child work is cancelled, and temporary storage is cleaned after required audit is retained. External side effects already confirmed by API read-back are reported as completed and enter an explicit compensation path rather than being represented as undone.

### Required proof before implementation is accepted

Implementation and E2E tickets must prove proposal fingerprint invalidation, all five evidence categories, missing/zero-test rejection, separate known-failure exceptions plus final activation approval, exact owner authentication, deterministic member-request authorization despite failure triage, progressive per-capability activation with single-version operations and mandatory policy/version audit fingerprint, crash-resume idempotency under immutable decision/revocation semantics, verified rollback restoration or exact accepted rollback-readiness failure, immediate post-opening probes, both LLM-confidence branches, shared 24-hour Approval Request expiry/outbox behavior, selective blocking, owner direct resume, first-wins owner rollback, exception expiry/revocation, in-flight draining, side-effect reconciliation, content-minimized audit, and zero cross-group leakage.
