# Prototype the Restricted Group Policy management experience

Type: prototype
Parent: [Design and launch reusable Restricted Group Policy](../map.md)
Blocked by: 02, 03

## Question

What should the owner see and do when creating, binding, inspecting, approving, revoking, disabling, rolling back, and auditing a Restricted Group Policy from local TUI and the exact owner control conversation? Build the cheapest interactive artifact that exposes ambiguous states and lets the owner react before the management contract is locked.
