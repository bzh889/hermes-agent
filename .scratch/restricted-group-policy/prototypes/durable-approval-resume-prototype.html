Durable Task contract: metadata-only state machine

      

        
ApprovalRequest.create(profile, type, decisionPayloadFingerprint, sourceFingerprint, policyRevision)
Creates one canonical profile-local request. No raw sensitive content persisted. Metadata only: identity, state, fingerprints, timestamps, bound policy revision.

        
ApprovalRequest.decide(surface, decision, authenticatedOwnerProof)
First authenticated owner decision wins. Later decisions on same request become resolved mirrors. Decision is immutable and idempotent under exact retry.

        
DurableTask.resume(currentAuthorityFn)
After restart, re-fetches versioned source content under current authority. Blocks or expires when source is unavailable or no longer authorized. Invalidates changed fingerprints.

        
ApprovalRequest.expire() | ApprovalRequest.stop(shortId)
24-hour expiry or explicit /stop command. Fail-closed block when source unavailable. Exact-once policy mutation with delivery reconciliation.
