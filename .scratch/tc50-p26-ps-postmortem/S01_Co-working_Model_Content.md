## Slide 1 revised content proposal — Re-cap. Established Co-working Model

**Title:** Re-cap. Established Co-working Model

**Subtitle:** P26 collaboration used a fixed weekly rhythm, supplemented by on-demand technical discussion

### Wednesday — PS weekly sync

**Fixed weekly forum**

- PS customization features
- Requirement / scope alignment
- Feature status and DRI
- Interface / validation boundary
- Follow-up and delivery tracking

### Friday — SIM weekly sync

**Fixed weekly forum**

- SIM / eSIM-related customization
- Requirement and feature status
- Implementation / issue follow-up
- Validation and customer dependency
- P26/P27 carry-forward

### On-demand small group

**No fixed frequency**

Triggered by:

- Important issue
- Technical gap
- Decision need
- Deep-dive or log review
- RCA / workaround discussion
- Test or owner alignment

Frequency is demand-driven: discussions may occur daily, and several sessions may be arranged on the same day when the issue requires it.

### Bottom-line message

> **Operating pattern: 2 fixed weekly forums + demand-driven technical collaboration**

The fixed meetings provide continuity; the small groups provide depth and faster convergence for specific topics.

### Speaker-note guardrail

Do not imply that small-group discussions have a fixed cadence or fixed count. The confirmed model is:

```text
Wednesday: PS weekly sync
Friday: SIM weekly sync
As needed: small-group technical discussions, potentially daily and multiple times per day
```
